    Our story

    Born in Kolkata in 1958

    Academic Publishers began with scholarly books in science, technology and medicine, and soon added management, sociology, economics and political science. Today it is one of the country's leading textbook publishers, with partners and distributors around the world.

    Our mission

    Quality books. Wider reach. Recognised authors.

    We publish books of the highest quality, carry scholarly work as far as it can go, and nurture talented authors so their work earns the recognition it deserves.

    What we do beyond publishing

    Book & magazine supply

    English and Bengali books and magazines for readers, associations and libraries worldwide.

    Self-publishing support

    Editing, publishing and distribution for authors who want to go it alone — with a team behind them.

    Pre-press & DTP

    Typesetting, composing and design in English, Bengali and Hindi — or complete turnkey production.

    Exhibitions abroad

    Showcases of Indian books and magazines across the world, especially Europe and North America.

    Journals & annual reports

    Printing and publishing for associations in Europe and North America.

    Global partnerships

    Co-publishing with WHO, FAO and ILO, and a member of Google's Book Partner Program.

    Reviewed by experts

    Subject committees assess every proposal and suggest how to make it stronger for the academic community.

    Rewarding royalties

    Rates that compare well with other publishers — and prices that keep your book within students' reach.

    A real person, every step

    Our in-house publishing professionals answer your questions from proposal to bound book.

    Fast to print

    Specialists prepare your electronic manuscript so it moves quickly into production.

    Quality under one roof

    Mark-up, printing and binding by our own staff — consistent quality, fewer costly errors.

    Promoted nationwide

    Direct mail, advertising and a network of bookstores and resellers that stock college titles.
